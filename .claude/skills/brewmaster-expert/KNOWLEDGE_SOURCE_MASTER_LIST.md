# Knowledge Source Master List v1.1

**Re-audited:** 2026-09-10

## Tiered by function, not by a universal score

### Foundational brewing theory
- How to Brew, 4th ed. — John Palmer.
- Scientific Principles of Malting and Brewing, 2nd ed. (2023) — Bamforth & Fox.
- Technology: Brewing and Malting, 6th ed. (2019) — Kunze / ed. Hendel.
- Water — Palmer & Kaminski.
- Yeast — White & Zainasheff.
- Malt — Mallett.
- For the Love of Hops — Hieronymus.

### Contemporary specialized practice
- Modern Lager Beer (2024) — Hendler & Connolly.
- Brewing with Wheat — Hieronymus.
- The New IPA — Janish; use as a research/practitioner synthesis and verify important mechanisms against primary/professional sources.
- Quality Management — Pellettieri.
- Brewery Safety — Stinchfield.

### Professional/scientific organizations
- MBAA Technical Quarterly.
- ASBC Methods of Analysis and Journal resources.
- EBC / Analytica-EBC.
- Brewers Association technical, quality, safety, packaging and draught resources.
- BJCP current beer style guidelines and errata.

### Current manufacturer/product sources
- Yeast: Lallemand, Fermentis, White Labs, Wyeast and relevant manufacturers.
- Malt: actual COA/lot + maltster specification.
- Hops: actual package/COA/lot; YCH lot analysis where applicable; supplier/breeder data.
- Equipment: exact model manual/datasheet and pressure/electrical ratings.

### Software and telemetry
- Brewfather current official docs/API/device integrations.
- KegLand/RAPT official docs where product/system-specific.

### Practitioner/discovery sources
- Qualified brewer interviews, technical manufacturer webinars and strong educational videos.
- Forums/Reddit/community: hypothesis/discovery only unless independently verified.

### Brewery-private evidence
- Calibrated equipment profile.
- Brewfather recipes and actual batch data.
- Manual OG/FG/pH/volume measurements.
- iSpindel/Pill/RAPT telemetry.
- Temperature/pressure history.
- Hop/malt/yeast lot data.
- Sensory/QC records.
- Cleaning/sanitation and packaging records.
- Controlled experiments and lessons learned.

See `references/source-registry.md` for audited URLs, version notes and known source conflicts.
