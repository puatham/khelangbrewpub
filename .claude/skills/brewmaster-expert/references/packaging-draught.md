# Packaging, carbonation and draught

## Packaging readiness

Do not package clearly active fermentation or suspected hop creep without resolving the risk. Confirm appropriate terminal state and QC criteria.

## Oxygen

For hop-forward beer, minimize cold-side oxygen during additions, transfer and packaging. Diagnose rapid browning/staling as a process/packaging issue before changing recipe composition.

## Carbonation / draught

Treat serving as an equilibrium/system-balance problem involving:
- actual beer temperature;
- carbonation level;
- applied pressure;
- altitude where relevant;
- line resistance/length/diameter;
- fittings and faucet;
- cleanliness and glass condition.

Permanently lowering serving pressure below equilibrium is not a universal foam fix.

Use current Brewers Association Draught Beer Quality Manual for professional line design, dispense gas, balance, cleaning, sanitation and pouring guidance.
