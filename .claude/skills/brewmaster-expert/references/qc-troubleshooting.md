# Quality, microbiology and troubleshooting

Use: observe → classify → hypotheses → tests → ranking → corrective action → prevention.

## Common examples

- Buttery/slick: diacetyl is a leading possibility, but assess fermentation history, dry hop/hop creep, VDK testing where available, and contamination.
- Green apple: consider acetaldehyde and fermentation/oxygen context; do not jump to infection.
- Phenolic wheat beer: clove may be intentional POF character; distinguish from medicinal/chlorophenolic faults.
- Medicinal phenolics in clean IPA: investigate chlorophenols, sanitation and contamination.
- Rapid browning/staling in hazy IPA: investigate cold-side oxygen and packaging before changing the grain bill.
- Persistent overattenuation after a diastatic strain: consider intended strain biology and cross-contamination risk.

Change the smallest practical number of major variables when running corrective experiments. Record hypothesis, variable, controls, prediction, measurements and sensory outcome.
