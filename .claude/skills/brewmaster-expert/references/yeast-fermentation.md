# Yeast and fermentation

## Product-specific data

Use current manufacturer information for temperature, attenuation, flocculation, STA1/diastatic status, alcohol tolerance and pitch guidance when material.

Examples checked 2026-09-10:
- LalBrew Verdant IPA: current product/TDS lists 75–82% attenuation, 18–25°C, medium flocculation, 12% ABV tolerance and 50–100 g/hL pitch rate.
- Wyeast 3068: current page lists 73–77% attenuation, 18–24°C, low flocculation, and notes that temperature, wort density and pitch rate influence banana/clove balance; overpitch can strongly suppress banana character.
- Fermentis WB-06: current page identifies Saccharomyces cerevisiae var. diastaticus and lists 50–80 g/hL, ideally 18–26°C.
- White Labs WLP066: current product page lists 75–82% attenuation, low-medium flocculation, 18–22°C and STA1 negative. An older tech PDF shows 17–21°C; treat this as a source-version conflict and prefer current live product information unless a newer TDS supersedes it.

## Fermentation state

Use multiple signals:
- current gravity and slope;
- expected attenuation / expected FG;
- temperature and history;
- yeast strain/pitch condition;
- pressure;
- elapsed time;
- dry-hop state;
- sensory/QC state;
- prior brewery history.

Calendar day is context, not the sole transition trigger.

## Sensor hierarchy

Floating hydrometers are highly useful for trends. For irreversible/high-impact transitions, confirm when uncertainty could matter. Investigate offsets if a calibrated manual measurement disagrees.

## Weizen rule

Do not tell the brewer that simply lowering temperature after two days “creates clove.” Banana ester and clove phenolic expression depend on strain biology, wort composition, mash/ferulic-acid conditions, pitch, oxygen, temperature and other process factors. Describe the trade-off instead of using a temperature myth.

## Completion

Airlock inactivity is not proof of completion. Before cold crash/packaging, assess stability, expected attenuation, yeast behavior, dry-hop/hop-creep state and trustworthy gravity evidence.
