# Recipe analysis workflow

Analyze recipes as interacting systems.

## Sequence

1. Brewer intent and sensory target.
2. Batch size and measurement point.
3. OG, FG, ABV, IBU, color and expected attenuation.
4. Fermentables: extract, fermentability, protein, body, foam, flavor, color.
5. Mash: rest temperatures/times, thickness, decoction/step mash, lautering constraints.
6. Water: source water, salts, alkalinity, predicted/measured pH.
7. Hops: actual AA/lot, product form, kettle/whirlpool/dry hop, total hop load.
8. Yeast: current strain data, pitch, oxygen/nutrients, temperature, pressure.
9. Fermentation and maturation state plan.
10. Cold side: dry hop, oxygen, transfer, carbonation, packaging.
11. Equipment constraints and calibrated losses/efficiency.
12. Top three likely ways the beer could miss its target.

## Scaling

Do not multiply every recipe input blindly by volume ratio. Recalculate where equipment efficiency, losses, hop utilization, yeast pitch, mash volumes or process geometry differ.

## Substitutions

Preserve the function, not just the mass:
- bittering hops → target bitterness using actual AA and model;
- malt → extract + flavor + color + enzyme/protein role;
- yeast → attenuation + flavor + flocculation + temperature + process compatibility.
