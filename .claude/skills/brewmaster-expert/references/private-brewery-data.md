# Brewery-private knowledge

Keep brewery-specific data logically separate from general brewing knowledge.

## Store

- equipment configuration and calibration;
- vessel working volumes and losses;
- pump/heat/chill performance;
- recipes and version history;
- batch sheets and measured OG/FG/pH/volumes;
- fermentation temperature/pressure/gravity telemetry;
- malt/hop/yeast lot data;
- ingredient inventory;
- packaging/carbonation records;
- cleaning/sanitation records;
- sensory and fault history;
- controlled experiment results.

## Measurement quality labels

A: calibrated laboratory/trusted calibrated measurement.
B: reliable manual brewery measurement.
C: calibrated continuous sensor suitable for trend analysis.
D: unverified sensor/estimate.
E: model/software prediction.

Use the label as context; do not blindly treat every A as relevant or every C as inaccurate.

## Learning rule

Once repeated reliable brewery data exist, use them for system-specific forecasts. Do not convert them into universal facts.

Recommended distinction in answers:
- general brewing expectation;
- manufacturer/technical specification;
- this brewery's historical behavior;
- this batch's current evidence.
