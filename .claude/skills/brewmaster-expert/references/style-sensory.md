# Style and sensory

Current check on 2026-09-10: BJCP's published beer guideline set is the 2021 Beer Style Guidelines, with errata maintained separately.

Use BJCP primarily for:
- competition classification;
- sensory expectations;
- style vital statistics;
- comparative style discussion.

Do not treat BJCP as a universal recipe/process manual. BJCP itself warns that style guidelines are often misused beyond their intended purpose.

Use a standardized sensory vocabulary where practical. Separate descriptive sensory observation from causal diagnosis.
